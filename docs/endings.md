# Ending guide — spoilers

## Continuing-journey edition (main-menu option 1)

This edition has the eight outcomes below plus **HONEST VICTORY** (defeat the
boss with ordinary combat; EARNED THE HARD WAY; 8 gold) and **PAID PERFORMANCE**
(journey 2+, pay the displayed bribe; PAY-TO-WIN HERO; no delivery pay or boss XP).
All outcomes allow another journey using the same character. Refusal retries
the current journey instead of unlocking the next one. See
[progression rules](progression.md) for XP, growth, recurring rewards and wagers.

The continuing boss uses 120–140 HP, 18–20 attack and 8 defence. Level 1 is
outmatched, but higher-level characters can win without Pip. Pip still turns
both the enemy and the delivery parcel into bread. The three rotating jobs use
a pan, curtains and a resignation form; their outcome structure stays the same.
This mode autosaves every decision, including final settlement. There is no
rollback checkpoint: loading cannot undo a defeat or a wager.

## Classic combat edition (main-menu option 7 / existing classic saves)

The combat edition has eight final endings. Each unlocks a different achievement.
Achievements belong to the current character's saved playthrough, not a global
collection. The names below describe outcomes, not labels shown before choices.

| Ending | How to reach it | Achievement | Delivery reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CLOCKED OUT | Refuse the parcel at the guild. | ANY% HERO | 0 gold |
| DELIVERY COMPLETE | Deliver an intact pan peacefully. | DELIVERY HERO | 5 gold |
| DISH DUTY | Deliver a dented pan without repairing it. | LORD OF THE RINSE | 0 gold; dinner and washing-up duty |
| ACCIDENTAL CATERING | Deliver the bread pan peacefully. | BREADWINNER | 5 gold |
| TOLL TAKEN | Reach zero HP while fighting the bridge slime. | SPOON-FED DEFEAT | 0 gold |
| BOSS DEFEAT | Insist on fighting the dragon and reach zero HP. | ONE-HIT INTERN | 0 gold |
| TACTICAL RETREAT | Enter the boss fight, then retreat. | CAREER PRESERVATION | 0 gold |
| THE FINAL LOAF | Bring Pip, insist on the boss fight, then ask Pip for help. | BREAD OF THE REALM | 0 gold; an enormous dragon loaf |

These are delivery rewards, not final wallet balances: starting gold, side
quests, purchases, tolls and the ghost's castle bonus can change the total.
Non-delivery endings keep that earlier balance rather than confiscating it.

## The new punchlines

- **SPOON-FED DEFEAT:** "You lost to cutlery. The guild has requested its sword back."
  The slime puts the hero in the recovery position and prints a defeat receipt.
- **ONE-HIT INTERN:** "Your sword dealt zero damage. Your confidence took 999."
  The dragon flicks the hero onto the welcome mat and records "Delivery attempted."
- **CAREER PRESERVATION:** "You chose a long life over a very short boss fight."
  Dinner is missed; survival is achieved. The pan is still the hero's problem.
- **BREAD OF THE REALM:** "You defeated the final boss. The kingdom now has a crust problem."
  The dragon loaf still wears an apron. Dinner is enormous; payment is pending
  because nobody can sign the receipt.

## Important distinctions

The Demon King has 9999 HP, 999 attack and 99 defence. No available weapon or
Bea attack can damage him. Bea can block one turn, but cannot win the fight.
Pip's transformation is the only boss victory. It transforms the pan as well,
even if it was already dented or bread. A repair kit cannot reverse it.

Asking for a fight and then choosing dinner still gives a normal delivery ending.
Only retreating **after entering combat** gives TACTICAL RETREAT. Retreating from
the bridge merely returns to crossing choices, preserving any damage taken.

Pip's bridge transformation also unlocks **BREAD OVER BRAWN**. This is an extra
encounter achievement, not a ninth ending. It can coexist with BREADWINNER or
BREAD OF THE REALM. Pip can use his spell once in each of the two encounters.

Every ending supports status, inventory, quests, journal, achievements, saving,
loading, returning to the character menu and quitting. A failed hero is not
deleted. Loading restores the last manual save; saving after defeat stores that
completed defeat, so it does not create a pre-battle checkpoint automatically.

Create a new character for these combat routes. Old characters labelled
`Original story` keep their four original endings and original decision sequence.
